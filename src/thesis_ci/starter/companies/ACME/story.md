# ACME: two-minute story

ACME Corp. is a fictitious company. It makes small parts that factories cannot run without, and a customer that
changes suppliers must recertify its production lines, so few customers leave [src:ACME-10K-FY2025#Item1].
About 60% of revenue recurs every year [src:ACME-10K-FY2025#Item7], and gross margin was 61% in
FY2025 [src:ACME-10K-FY2025#Item8].

Where this is most likely wrong: a new open standard could make its parts interchangeable with cheaper ones.
